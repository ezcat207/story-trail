# The One-Sentence Story: Your Compass
*4 min read*

**The picture:** A compass does not show every tree. It shows which way is north. Your one-sentence story does the same. When you are lost in chapter six, it tells you which way the story is going.

**The formula**
> When **[something happens]**, a **[kind of hero]** must **[goal]** before **[deadline or cost]**, but **[what stands in the way]**.

Not every story fits it exactly, but fill in the blanks and you will find out fast whether you have a story or just a cool setting.

**Test your sentence**
- Is there a **person** with a **want**? (Not "a world with magic.")
- Is there something **blocking** the want?
- Is there a **cost** if they fail?
- Could a stranger repeat it back in one breath?

**Percy Jackson check:** When a god's weapon is stolen and Percy is blamed, he must find it and return it within ten days before the gods start a war, but monsters and a traitor are in his way. One breath. Hero, want, clock, blockers.

**Try it (20 min):** Write your sentence three times, each with a different blocker. Pick the one that makes you want to know what happens.
