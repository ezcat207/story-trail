# Start Here: How This Guide Works
*3 min read*

Think of writing a short story like planning a road trip. You can wander and hope for the best, or you can look at a map first. A map will not drive the car for you. You still pick the music, the snacks, and the weird detour. But it stops you from ending up in a swamp at midnight.

This guide is that map, written for writers 13 and up. It has 14 short chapters. You do not have to read them in order. Each one answers one question writers get stuck on.

**How each chapter works**
- **The picture:** a comparison to something from real life, so the idea sticks.
- **The rules:** the idea in plain words.
- **Percy Jackson check:** how *The Lightning Thief* uses it. (Spoilers ahead. If you have not read it, the short version: a kid finds out he is a demigod and must clear his name and stop a war between gods in ten days.)
- **Try it (20 min):** one small thing to do. Reading about writing does not make you better. Writing does.

**Three promises**
1. Short stories are the training ground. They are small enough to finish, and finishing is a skill.
2. You are the author. Use AI as a coach that points at problems, never as the one who writes your story.
3. A bad first draft is normal. A finished bad draft beats a perfect first page every time.
