# The Three-Act Map
*5 min read*

**The picture:** A story is like a rollercoaster. First you climb and get strapped in. Then comes the long ride with drops, loops, and turns. Then the big final drop and the slow roll back to the station.

**The map**
- **Act 1 (about the first 10–25%): Setup.** Show normal life, then break it (the **inciting incident**). End with the hero making a choice they cannot undo. That is the **first turning point**.
- **Act 2 (about the middle 50–60%): Confrontation.** Things get harder. New friends, new enemies, bigger stakes. Halfway through, something big changes the game. It ends at the lowest point, when all seems lost.
- **Act 3 (about the last 15–25%): Resolution.** The hero takes the final big action (the climax), and then the story settles (the ending).

**A tool for scenes: LOCK**
- **L**ead: who is the hero here?
- **O**bjective: what do they want in this scene?
- **C**onfrontation: what stops them?
- **K**nockout: how does the scene end, in a way that changes things?

**Percy Jackson check:** Act 1: Percy is attacked, learns about the gods, and heads out on the quest. Act 2: the road trip, each stop a challenge. The Oracle's prophecy hints at betrayal. Dark moment: the Underworld and the lost bolt. Act 3: the fight on Mount Olympus, the traitor revealed, and the return home.

**Try it (20 min):** Draw three boxes for the acts. Write your first turning point (the choice that closes the door behind your hero).
