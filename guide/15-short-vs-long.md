# Short Story vs. Novel
*3 min read*

**The picture:** A short story is a sprint. A novel is a marathon. Same sport, different training.

**Differences**
- **Size:** a short story has one main problem, few characters, and a tight timeline. A novel has room for subplots and side characters.
- **Speed:** short stories start close to the trouble and end soon after the climax.
- **Focus:** every scene in a short story has to earn its spot.
- **Ending:** short stories can end on a single sharp image or twist.

**Why start with short stories:** You can finish one in a week. Finishing teaches more than starting ten novels.

**Percy Jackson check:** The book is a novel with many episodes. Try writing one episode of it, such as a single monster encounter, as a stand-alone short story. You will see all the techniques at small scale.

**Try it (20 min):** Pick one chapter of a book you love. Write its one-sentence story. Notice that it works as a story on its own.
