# Story Trail

A free, single-page map for teen writers (13+): twelve stations that take you from a first spark to a finished short story, a **Percy Jackson case study**, and a **copy-paste AI prompt** that scores your story out of 100 against a clear rubric.

- Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no tracking. English and 中文.
- `prompts/rubric-prompt.en.md` and `prompts/rubric-prompt.zh.md`: paste into ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI chat, then paste your story under it.
- `index.template.html` + `build.py`: the editable source. Run `python3 build.py` to rebuild `index.html`.

## Stations
0 Collect Sparks · 1 One-Sentence Story · 2 Know Your Hero · 3 Blueprint · 4 The Opening · 5 The Middle: Dominoes · 6 The Climax: Charged Attack · 7 The Ending: Two Locks · 8 Conflict · 9 Dialogue and Voice · 10 Teaser and Chapter Hooks · 11 Final Boss: Revise and Score

## Credits and honesty notes
- Craft ideas are inspired by 《让写作成为你的副业：短篇故事创作及变现攻略》 by 北海 (开明出版社, 2025). This repository contains an original rewrite for young writers with its own examples. It does **not** include the book’s text, and it leaves out the book’s market and income chapters. Please buy the book if you want the full version.
- Percy Jackson examples refer to *The Lightning Thief* by Rick Riordan. Plot points were written from memory and public summaries. Please check them against your own copy. Sample dialogue marked “imitation” is original to this project.
- The AI scoring prompt is a coaching tool, not an official grade. Scores are only as good as the AI and the rubric. A normal good first draft lands in the 60–75 range.
